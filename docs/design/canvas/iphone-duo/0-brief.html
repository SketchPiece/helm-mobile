Two displays, one ratio

outer 5.4″
~466×678 pt
inner 7.6″, fold in the middle
~890×626 pt = two outers

The inner display is exactly two outer displays side by side (√2, like A4). Closed, you hold a phone; open, you hold a book with two pages, each about the size of the outer screen.

Poses

Closedouter, compact
Bookhalf open, fold divides
Desktop shows, bottom taps
Openflat, regular width

Cameras

A front camera on each display; the inner one sits under the panel and only "appears" while active. With the rear camera running, the outer display faces whoever is being filmed.

Apple's rules for every app

  • Resize, don't redesign per pose. Compact width outside, regular width inside; size classes, margins, safe areas. No UIScreen.main, no orientation checks.
  • Bars go to the side. Toolbars, tab bar, status bar and the Dynamic Island stack on a vertical rail (outer display and inner landscape). Back/Close on top, then Done; icons with titles; overflow by priority.
  • Same functions in every pose. Inside may add one level of hierarchy (Mail: list + message), never a different app.
  • Keep controls off the fold. Buttons, sheets, alerts get displaced to a page; scrolling content may cross it.
  • Desk pose is the one optional special layout: media up, controls on the stable bottom half.

What it means for Helm today

Helm Mobile is iPhone-only, one NavigationStack, no size classes anywhere, custom bottom bar. Built with the 27.1 SDK it would extend under the rail; without work it shows as a stretched phone inside. Step 1 is plain hygiene.

New capabilities

  • Hinge angle, live. onHingeChange / UIHingeInteraction: closed / partially open / open plus continuous degrees. Apple's demo: a whammy bar.
  • Fold as a reserved region. reservedRegions(kind: .division) tells where the pages are.
  • Arrangement views. ArrangementView split or overlay: two views that go side by side, stacked, or onto separate pages when folded.
  • Multiple windows. First iPhone with several scenes of one app, side by side, inner display only.
  • Scene accessories. Extra UI on another display; on Duo the CameraCaptureAccessory shows content on the outer screen while the camera runs inside.
