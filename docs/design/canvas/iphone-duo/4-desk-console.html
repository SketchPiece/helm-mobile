Idea B · desk pose · onHingeChangeStand it on the desk: a console for your agentsHalf-open on the table, the top half is a screen you read from a step away and the bottom half is a remote you tap without lifting the phone. It is today's Mac remote, but it no longer needs the Mac in front of you: put it beside the laptop on a call, on the kitchen counter, next to a second monitor. Apple names this pose as the one special layout worth making.
Enter by folding to ~90–120° with the phone resting on its edge. Lifting it flat or closing it hands the same thread back to the book or the outer screen, nothing lost.
Voice first. In this pose the mic listens for "Helm, …" hands-free, so you can talk to a running agent while typing on the Mac.
