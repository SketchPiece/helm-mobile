Today

  • TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1: iPhone only. On an iPad it runs in compatibility mode, a phone-shaped window in the middle of the screen.
  • Portrait only (Info.plist). On an iPad in landscape on a Magic Keyboard, the app is turned sideways or letterboxed.
  • One NavigationStack: Home → thread, with everything else in sheets.
  • No horizontalSizeClass or NavigationSplitView anywhere.

What we already have that helps

  • Every screen is SwiftUI, and the composer and timeline already lay themselves out by width.
  • Image drop on the composer already works, so drag and drop on iPad comes for free.
  • The screen remote already has a landscape design (screen-remote canvas).
