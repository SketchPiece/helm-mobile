Layout

1The sidebar is Home, as on the iPhone: project chips, Needs you / Pinned / Active, then the Snoozed and Settled shelves. New thread is the amber button in the sidebar header.
2The open thread stays highlighted in amber. Context menus and swipes on the rows work as on the phone.
3The toolbar has the title and «project · branch · Mac», then the context meter, Review changes (inspector), and ⋯.
4The message column is 680pt and centered in the space beside the sidebar. Content scrolls under the glass sidebar.
5The composer has the same width as the column and works as on the phone. With a hardware keyboard, ⏎ sends.

Why this one

  • Same mental model as the iPhone and the Mail pattern Apple suggests.
  • In a compact window NavigationSplitView collapses into today's iPhone app, with nothing extra to build.
  • Rows, composer, timeline: everything is reused.

Cost

  • Projects are only a filter (chips), not a tree like on the Mac.
