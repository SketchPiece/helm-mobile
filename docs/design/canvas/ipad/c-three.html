Structure

  • Column 1: smart lists (All, Needs you, Pinned), projects, shelves, the Mac and Settings.
  • Column 2: threads in the chosen list.
  • Column 3: the thread. On 11" it's left with ~580pt, and the column is 580 instead of 680.

For

  • Like Mail with mailboxes: projects and shelves always in sight.
  • Fits a 13" in landscape well.

Against

  • On 11" and in portrait the chat gets cramped. Most of the time column 1 would be hidden anyway, which makes it A.
  • The phone has no such level, so a compact window collapses three steps instead of two.
  • The main thing here is the conversation, not navigation, and the conversation is what loses space.
