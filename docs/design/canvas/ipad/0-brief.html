What Apple says (HIG + WWDC25 «Elevate the design of your iPad app»)

  • Windows can be any size. In iPadOS 26 the opt-out key UIRequiresFullScreen is deprecated; with the iOS 27 SDK it stops working. Every width from Slide Over to a full 13" screen has to work.
  • Choose the layout by size class, not by device or orientation. A bigger window shows more (sidebar, inspector, buttons out of the ⋯ menu), but the app does the same things. Resizing must be reversible.
  • Deep or nested content gets a sidebar (Mail, Notes). Columns collapse into one stack when the width is compact. Keep the current selection highlighted.
  • Content runs under the glass sidebar and toolbar (backgroundExtensionEffect, scroll edge effect).
  • Window controls (●●●) sit at the leading edge of the toolbar, and toolbar items move right to make room. Every window gets a clear title, which for us is the thread's title.
  • Every app has a menu bar (swipe down, or the pointer at the top edge). List every action, by how often it's used, with SF Symbols. Disable items, never hide them. The View menu has the sidebar and its shortcut.
  • Pointer: hover highlights are drawn with glass. Test every control with a trackpad, and never put anything important behind hover alone.
  • Keyboard: standard shortcuts stay standard. ⌘ is the main modifier and ⇧ the second. ⌃ is the system's, except for standards like ⌃⌘S.
  • Inspector (.inspector): a column on the right when wide, a sheet when compact. It also gives a menu item and a shortcut.

How similar apps do it

  • Mail / Messages: the list of conversations in a sidebar, the open one in the detail column, the selection highlighted. A narrow window gets the iPhone stack. That's Helm's model one to one: Home is the list, the thread is the detail.
  • ChatGPT / Claude (desktop and web): chats in a sidebar, a centered message column, and the composer at the bottom of the column. ⌘N new chat, sidebar toggle by shortcut, Return sends. Their 2026 iPad layouts I couldn't check.
  • Slack: ⌘K quick switcher to any conversation, ⌘/ shows the shortcuts. Checked on desktop.
  • Helm Desktop (Mac): projects with their threads in the sidebar, the thread on the right, diffs in a side pane. The iPad sits between the phone and this.
  • Jump Desktop / Screens (remote desktop): a trackpad and mouse drive the remote pointer (right-click, scroll, drag) and keyboard shortcuts pass through. API: prefersPointerLocked + GCMouse deltas, which only work in full screen or in the front window.
  • Blink Shell: remappable shortcuts (vim and emacs collide with ⌘ and ⌃), and Esc mapped onto a key since iPad keyboards have none.

What that means for Helm

  • NavigationSplitView: Home in the sidebar, the thread in the detail column. Compact widths collapse to today's iPhone app exactly. Never branch on idiom.
  • Message column ≈ 680pt, centered, and the composer the same width. Lines don't stretch across 1100pt.
  • Review changes → an inspector on the right, instead of a sheet over the chat.
  • With a hardware keyboard: ⏎ sends, ⇧⏎ adds a line, ⌘. stops the agent, ⌘K jumps anywhere. Detect the keyboard with GCKeyboard; without one, the iPhone behaviour.
  • A menu bar through .commands: File / Thread / View / Go. The shortcuts come with it.
  • Several windows: a thread in its own window, and the screen remote in its own window. Each window titled after its thread.
  • Screen remote: the iPad trackpad becomes the Mac's pointer one to one, a finger is direct touch, and ⌘ shortcuts go to the Mac.
  • The AGENTS.md rules still hold (no interactive glass around menus, Equatable menus, 44pt targets). The first tap is also checked with the trackpad.
