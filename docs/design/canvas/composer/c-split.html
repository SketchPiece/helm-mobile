C. Glass pill and a separate amber button beside it (iOS 26 Messages style)

The action leaves the pill and becomes its own 48 pt amber drop to the right. The pill only holds text and attachments. Typing: the drop turns into send, the mic slides into the pill's right end. With GlassEffectContainer the two shapes melt into each other as they move.
