No hero, every thread is an equal row: the state glyph, the title, the project under it, and the timer in a fixed slot. A thread that needs you gets a tinted background and goes first. That reads faster than a separate block.
No header with the logo and counts. The logo moves to a small corner mark, and the space goes to a third row. Fits in 160 pt: 3 rows and «+2 more».
The compact Dynamic Island: the glyph of the most important state on the left, a row of pips on the right, one per thread in its colour. Its width only changes when a thread is added, not every second.
The cost: the pips are small, and the timer isn't visible in the island.
