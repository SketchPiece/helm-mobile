B. Computers first (recommended)

The computers are what the app is for, so they open the screen as cards: OS icon (laptop / server), status, OS and version, and the agents that are ready on it. Work becomes three tiles with live numbers. Tailscale moves into each computer's Connection section, where it actually matters. Everything below stays a tidy list.
